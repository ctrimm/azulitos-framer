**Name**
Azulitos

**Byline**
Portuguese tiles, made to repeat

**Price:** **Free.**

**Description**
Little Portuguese tiles for Framer — 72 of them, in the manner of traditional azulejos. Most are padrão pattern tiles: each one carries a quarter of the design, so when you repeat a tile across a frame, the quarters meet and the full rosettes appear, the way a real tile wall works.

What's inside:
• 48 padrão tiles — seamless, quarter-motif designs that repeat into panels
• 16 medalhão tiles — standalone medallions for single use
• 8 polícromo tiles — pattern tiles in the Lisbon palette of cobalt, yellow, green and manganese
• One Color variable (Azul, default cobalt #1E4FA3) drives every cobalt tile — recolor the whole set from a single control
• All 72 tiles as SVG files, plus transparent PNGs at 1000px and 2000px, free to download (link below)

How to use them:
1. Paste the Vectors URL into your project — the set lands in Assets.
2. Drop a tile into a frame and repeat it (a 4×4 grid is a good start) to build a panel.
3. Change the Azul variable to recolor every cobalt tile at once.
4. The tiles are the painted motifs on transparency — put any ground colour behind them; white is the classic.

Stated plainly: the designs are original, generated from traditional motif grammar — stars, rosettes, quatrefoils, lace bands. They are in the manner of azulejos, not copies of historical panels.
